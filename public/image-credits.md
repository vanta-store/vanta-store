# VANTA product imagery

All product photographs in `public/images/` are local copies supplied by the project owner in `Clothes_Organized.zip`.

The source archive organized its photographs into these groups: bootcut jeans, straight-leg jeans, wide-leg jeans, trousers, long-sleeve Henley shirts, short-sleeve Henley shirts, other long-sleeve tops, button-up shirts, leather and suede jackets, and other jackets/coats.

Two exact duplicate pairs were intentionally excluded from the live catalogue: the duplicate black straight-leg jeans photo and duplicate medium-wash bootcut jeans photo. The 39 unique images are used in the shop, category tiles, home editorial areas, product detail pages, cart, and checkout preview.

No remote stock-photo image URLs are used by the storefront.
